# Practise with Codespaces

You do not have to install anything on your computer. A codespace is a ready-made workplace in the browser, and your AI assistant is already in it. You say what you want to practise for, and it makes the app.

[To the template on GitHub](https://github.com/lernapps/start) · [To your codespaces](https://github.com/codespaces) · [Rather practise with an assistant on your own computer?](index.md)

## How it works {#steps}

You need a free GitHub account and a subscription or a key for an AI assistant: Claude (Claude Pro or higher) or ChatGPT (with Codex). You pay for the AI yourself.

1. Open the template on GitHub (github.com/lernapps/start). Click “Use this template”, then “Create a new repository”. Give it a short name, for example “maths-test-fractions”.
2. Open your new repository. Click “Code”, then “Codespaces” and “Create codespace on main”. This takes about two minutes.
3. The editor asks whether you trust the authors of the files in this folder. Click “Trust folder and continue”. Without this yes the extensions for Claude and Codex stay off.
4. Sign in to your assistant. The guide with pictures shows how: [signing in to Claude](https://lernapps.net/apps/en/build/codespaces/#claude) and [signing in to ChatGPT (Codex)](https://lernapps.net/apps/en/build/codespaces/#chatgpt).
5. Tell the assistant that you want to practise for an occasion, for example “for the maths test next week”. Give it photos of the worksheets, with the subject, the grade and what is covered. It puts the app in this folder and checks it.
6. Submit the content and get the link to the app: [Submitting step by step](submit.md).

## Pausing and continuing later {#pause}

By default a codespace stops by itself after 30 minutes without input. A codespace you do not open for 30 days is deleted. Your files stay when it stops. To continue, open [your codespaces](https://github.com/codespaces) and click “Open” at your codespace. Do not create a new one: it would be empty. More, also on costs and data: [Build with Codespaces](https://lernapps.net/apps/en/build/codespaces/#pausieren).

The assistant may do anything in your codespace and sees the whole internet. Do not type passwords or data of pupils there. Content and app are public, so without names and school.
