To the start page: App made to measure

Practise with Codespaces

You do not have to install anything on your computer. A codespace is a ready-made workplace in the browser, and your AI assistant is already in it. You say what you want to practise for, and it makes the app.

The guide with pictures is with the apps, because it is almost the same for building and for practising. The steps marked “Practise” are yours:

To the guide: Build and practise with Codespaces

In short

  1. Open the template lernapps/start and make a repository of your own from it. It is only your workplace and can go afterwards.
  2. Start a codespace in it and sign in to your assistant.
  3. Tell it what you want to practise for, and give it photos of the worksheets.
  4. It gives you the ZIP file with the folder src. Submit it and get the link to the app: Submitting step by step.

You need a free GitHub account and a subscription or a key for Claude or ChatGPT. You pay for the AI yourself. Rather practise with an assistant on your own computer?

lernapps.net is free and has no ads. It lives on your thanks.
