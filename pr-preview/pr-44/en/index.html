Practise with exactly the material that will come up

Your AI assistant turns the worksheets into an app to practise with. Copy this sentence into a new conversation:

Read https://lernapps.net/practice/llms.txt and help me make an app to practise for <occasion>.

For example: “for my daughter's maths test next week” or “for a cover lesson in English, year 7”.

  1. Set the scope

    Photos of the worksheets, plus subject, year and what will come up.

  2. Your assistant does this for you

    Explanations and exercises, made for exactly this material, in a few minutes.

  3. Practise

    With a link, on any device, without an account. Try the RE app (in German) →

What you need

  • An AI assistant that can write software, such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini. It often comes with a subscription you already have.
  • A free GitHub account. Through GitHub you submit the content and get the link to the app.

Content and app are public, so leave out names and the school. See a real conversation · Submitting step by step · Privacy
